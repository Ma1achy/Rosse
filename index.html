About Rosse

Rosse is the drawing engine behind Almagest, a study of what a self-supervised encoder learns about galaxies. Every galaxy is built in three dimensions first: a bulge and a disc, arms with their spurs and clumps, a bar, rings, dust and a halo of stars. Then it is drawn from a library of real hand drawings, placed and inked by the graphics card, or by the processor when the browser has no WebGPU.

It is named after William Parsons, the third Earl of Rosse, who in 1845 became the first person to see a galaxy’s spiral arms, and drew them.
