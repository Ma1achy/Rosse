Vector pen lines on WebGPU: ribbons vs distance fields

Throwaway spike. Drawing whole.json vec[0] (AB-1, barred spiral) at 48, 220 and 1100 px, pen 2.4 px (half-width 1.2 px) constant in plate pixels. Panels: Canvas2D reference; A ribbons as in the reference (0.9w overlap, 4× MSAA); A2 ribbons with a capsule distance in the fragment shader; B unsigned distance field baked at 512² and 2048²; A3/B3 the same under the non-affine rewind warp. GPU panels are rendered offscreen and read back, then composited on paper. Timing runs afterwards and can take a minute.
