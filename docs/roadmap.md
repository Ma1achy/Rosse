# Roadmap

The roadmap runs from an empty canvas to parity with v21. Each milestone is one reviewable pull request, or a short series of them, and ends in something visible with a test that passes.

Some rules apply to every milestone from M1 on:

- Every compute kernel lands **with its CPU twin and a parity test** (ADR 0011, 0014).
- Every milestone that adds a feature adds its golden cases to the required set. Earlier cases must keep passing.
- Goldens use the metric of ADR 0013: (a) total ink, (b) structure SSIM on density maps, (c) pen weight, (d) mark counts, and (e) bit-exact against the engine's own SwiftShader goldens.

Effort is in focused working days for one engineer familiar with WebGPU, including the CPU twin. It assumes ADR 0014 stays at K1.

## Reading the acceptance column

"Goldens pass" means: for every listed preset × seeds {7, 4242} × cameras {home, orbit}, tests (a)–(d) at the parity thresholds pass against `tests/golden/reference/`, and (e) bit-exact against the engine's own goldens once they exist. It also means the CPU engine passes (a)–(d) at the strict thresholds against the WebGPU render.
