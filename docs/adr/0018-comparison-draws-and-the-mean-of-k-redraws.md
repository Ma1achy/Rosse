# 18. What the golden comparison draws with, and the mean of K re-draws

Date: 2026-10-06

## Status

Proposed, awaiting the owner's sign-off. Supersedes in part [0015](0015-golden-metric-as-calibrated-in-m2.md): its items 5 (the reference's variation) and 6 (calibration), and the first item of its future work. What is not changed here still holds as 0015 states it.

## Context

ADR 0015 compares the engine with v21 on the premise that **only the dots differ**: the runner draws with v21's replayed variation, and the thresholds are calibrated on re-draws that re-key only the placement stream. M4's line-work breaks that premise in three places, and the single-draw comparison showed a weakness of its own:

- **More discrete random choices.** v21 picks each curve's stroke row and which spurs are drawn (`curves()`, `mulberry32(VAR.strokeSeed)`); its lanes draw each hatch's offset, angle, length and the odd feather from `mulberry32(seed·733 + 29)` (app23.js:L951), each hatch's pen line from `rrL` (L1047), and the pen lines that carve the stipple from `rd` (L201); the ring knots' clusters (angle, radius, how many marks) come from `rr0` (L283–288). The engine draws all of these from its own counter streams (ADR 0004). M4's review (major M-1) found that the dust and ring-knot choices were neither replayed from v21 nor re-keyed by the calibration (they were keyed by the seed), so the comparison carried random differences the thresholds were never calibrated on.
- **Noise.** v21's `vnoise` and the engine's lattice noise share the interpolation and differ in their corner values (deliberate divergence 5), so flocculence, patchiness, the lanes' gaps and the hand wobble follow another pattern. Without v21's corners, `Flocculent` and `Hand wobble` at the zoom camera scored a coarse SSIM of 0.82–0.86: the metric sees the pattern, which is the divergence, not a defect.
- **The zoom camera** (M3, M4): at zoom 2 the plate holds the inner 2.4 units, and the same galaxy re-drawn scatters more there (coarse SSIM p5 0.87 against 0.91 at home).
- **One engine draw against one v21 draw.** Each case compared one engine draw with v21's. `Tightly wound` seed 4242 at zoom 2 failed on r50 (+6.9% against ±4.3%) while the same configuration re-drawn with 10 placement keys ranged from 0 to +6.9%: the canonical key was the extreme of the ten. A band calibrated on pairs of single draws judges the draw, not the engine.

Two more facts shape the decision. The engine's ring-knot clusters held 5–12 marks uniformly, but v21 draws the bound of its marks loop afresh at every turn (`jk < 5 + floor(rr0() · 8)`), so a v21 cluster holds 5 marks with probability 1/8 and 12 rarely: a difference of statistics, not only of draws. And each hatch's pen line was drawn on its index in the hatch list, so a change of `dustScribble` or `ring` re-rolled every later hatch (review minor m2).

## Decision

1. **The comparison draws with v21's choices** (`node.ts` `referenceOptions`), each checked against v21's own code cut out of app23.js and evaluated as written:
   - the variation, replayed offline (0015 item 5, `compare/v21.ts`);
   - the stroke picks (`SceneOptions.curvePicks`, from v21's own `curves()`, `compare/v21-curves.ts`), with the outline arcs' and the tidal tail's angles, which v21 draws from the same stream: the engine's curves then equal v21's control points to 10⁻⁹, every role included;
   - the noise corners (`SceneOptions.noise`, `compare/v21-noise.ts`, `NoiseField` and `common/noise-table.wgsl`). The lattice noise's own statistics are tested against v21's separately (mean, spread, tail fractions);
   - the dust choices (`SceneOptions.dustPicks`, `v21DustPicks`): v21's `dustLanes()` is evaluated with its stream recorded, so the engine gets every number in the order v21 used it (a Gaussian as `gauss()` returned it), with `rrL`'s pen line per hatch and `rd`'s per carving line. The engine's loops draw in v21's order, and with these numbers its hatches equal v21's strokes (position, angle and length to 10⁻⁶ px, at zoom 1 and 2, `tests/unit/lines.test.ts`);
   - the ring-knot clusters (`SceneOptions.ringKnotPicks`, `v21RingKnots`): `rr0` replayed with v21's own `mulberry32` and `gauss`, consuming the 8 numbers of each mark and the centre star's one, for each cluster's angle, radius and mark count.

   With them, the random choices the two engines make differently are v21's, and only the marks differ (their positions, kinds, pools, sizes and rotations). One exception is left: the number of drawn stars at each clump, which v21 draws from its main stipple stream, interleaved with the dots, and so cannot be replayed apart from them. It is the engine's, keyed by the placement key (item 2), and only counted (the vector stars are M5 and M7); with `starMix: 0`, as in the M4 set, there are none.
2. **The engine's placement key re-draws every choice that is not structure.** Besides the stipple, the key (`SceneOptions.placementKey`, the seed by default) now keys the ring-knot clusters, the clumps' drawn stars, and the lanes' own draws: each hatch's numbers and pen line, and the carving lines' pen lines. Which hatches exist stays with the noise, a structural choice. The stroke picks and the outline and tail angles stay with the seed, as v21 keeps them with the variation (`VAR.strokeSeed`). Each hatch draws its pen line from its own step's stream, after its offset, angle and length and before a feather's numbers, so it depends on neither the hatches before it nor `dustScribble` (`DustIndex.tiles` is retired). The engine draws its ring-knot counts as v21 does, the bound afresh at every turn, which a unit test checks against the exact distribution.
3. **The zoom camera has its own thresholds** (`<family>@zoom` in thresholds.json), calibrated by the same rule on the 11 calibration presets at zoom 2 plus the zoom captures.
4. **v21 is compared with the mean of K engine draws.** For each required case, every measure of (a), (b), (b′), (c) and (f) is the mean over K draws of the comparison of that draw with v21's capture: the engine's canonical draw (key 0, the engine's own) and K − 1 re-draws with keys seed + k · 7,919,000 (k = 1..K − 1). Means are arithmetic, except the position angles, which are averaged on the axis (twice the angle on the circle); counts are the mean count per class. The re-draws are rendered by the CPU engine, in parallel processes before the main loop: CPU = WebGPU at L1 is proven for every case by the strict comparison, so the WebGPU row is the mean of WebGPU's canonical draw and the same CPU re-draws. The canonical draw alone is printed for information, and the diff reports show its images. L0, the engine hashes (test e) and the strict L1 comparison use the canonical draw only.
5. **The thresholds are calibrated for that statistic.** Per configuration, each of R = 3 stand-ins for v21 (keys from 1000, apart from any K) is compared with the mean over the K draws of keys 0..K − 1, the comparison's own keys. Every negative control is drawn with the same K keys and compared, as a mean, with the first stand-in. The rules are 0015's: 1.5 × the 95th percentile of the re-draw spread (with ink and widths floored at ±5% and ±10%), the coarse SSIM at its 5th percentile minus 0.02, and per-preset axis-ratio tolerances. thresholds.json records K (`keys`), and the runner uses it.
6. **K = 6.** v21's capture is one draw, so the statistic's noise does not shrink as 1/√K, as 0015's future work assumed: with σ the noise of one draw, it is σ · √(1 + 1/K), against σ · √2 for a pair of single draws. That factor is 1.118 for K = 4, 1.080 for K = 6, 1.061 for K = 8 and 1 in the limit: K = 6 is within 8% of what any K can reach, K = 8 gains 2% more for a third more renders, and K = 4 leaves 12%. The calibration's measured spread agrees: the 95th percentile of |Δr50| for the spiral family falls from 2.80% at K = 1 to 1.91% at K = 2 and stays at 1.83–1.93% for K = 2–6; for `spiral@zoom` it falls from 3.59% to 2.23% at K = 4 and 2.10–2.14% at K = 5–6 (docs/milestones/m4/README.md). Beyond K = 6 the remaining noise is v21's own draw.
7. **Line-work alone is calibrated on held-out v21 captures as well.** The line-work-only captures (variant `lines`, ADR 0020) are drawn the same way at every placement key once the dust choices are replayed, so their re-draw spread is almost nil, and the rule of item 5 alone would gate them on the renderers' own differences (capsules against MSAA quads before ADR 0019, mip selection, f16 accumulation), which re-draws never show. For that family (`lines`), seeds 3, 11, 23 and 101 of every `lines` preset and camera are captured for the calibration only (`"calibration": true`, never gated), the same rule is applied to v21 against the mean of the engine's K draws there, and each threshold is the wider of the two (the coarse SSIM the lower). With 56 held-out configurations the tails are still heavy for the measures that jump with an aperture (a sparse drawing's axis ratio within r50 changes by 0.05 when one stroke crosses it, while the images agree to a coarse SSIM of 0.993), so no tolerance is set below 1.1 times the largest held-out value. The gated seeds, 7 and 4242, are out of sample.

## Consequences

- The bands are narrower than 0015's, by about √((1 + 1/K)/2) ≈ 0.76 where the noise dominates, and a single engine draw at the edge of its scatter no longer decides a case. What remains is v21's own draw, which no number of engine draws can average away.
- A structural change is judged against a quieter engine side. On the same measured configurations, 42 of the 44 control–family cells are caught as often or more often than with one draw (single draws aggregated from the same data, K′ = 1): 335 detections of 538 against 328; two cells lose one detection each (`smooth` halo off 3 → 2 of 17, `spiral@zoom` RMAX 4.2 5 → 4 of 17), where the one draw had crossed the band by its own scatter. The cells, the spreads by K and the thresholds are in `docs/data/m4-calibration-k.json` (written by `tools/m4-summary.mjs calibration`), summarised in docs/milestones/m4/README.md.
- A golden run renders and measures each required case K + 1 times on the CPU (the canonical draw and the engine's own variation besides). The re-draws run in parallel processes (`--jobs`, default 4); see the M4 results for the times.
- A calibration renders (R + K) draws per configuration plus K per applicable negative control: hours on four cores. Its shards are written after every configuration and a stopped run resumes (`--resume`); `--reuse-shards --keys K'` re-aggregates for any K' ≤ K without rendering. M4's calibration ran the negative controls on 73 of the 142 configurations (about every other one, `controls` in calibration.json; a full pass was started and stopped when it would have taken ten hours on the shared four cores), while the threshold pairs ran on all 142. A default `--calibrate` runs the controls on every configuration, and `--resume --controls-every 1` adds them to configurations whose pairs are done.
- **What this calibration does not do well, plainly.** Against the single-draw calibration it replaces (commit 6eb2e8e), several controls are caught less often, and some thresholds rose (`vsPrevious` in `docs/data/m4-calibration-k.json` lists all of them). The controls that lose most are the ones ADR 0015 already says the per-measure gates cannot reliably see (a halo missing, a thicker disc, a bulge a little bigger or flatter): `spiral` bulgeSize ×1.5 4/28 → 0/15, `spiral@zoom` bulgeFlat −0.15 2/12 → 0/5 and bulgeSize ×1.5 5/12 → 1/5, `smooth` halo off 8/32 → 2/17, `smooth@zoom` halo off 7/12 → 0/5 and RMAX 4.2 2/12 → 0/5, and `smooth@zoom` bulgeFlat +0.1 7/8 → 3/4. The K-mean is not the cause: the same shards aggregated as single draws catch no more of the zoom cells (`smooth@zoom` halo off and RMAX 0/5 both ways). The causes are the smaller sample (the controls ran on half the configurations, so a `@zoom` cell holds 2 to 17 configurations), and thresholds re-derived on other draws: `smooth@zoom` r50 0.033 → 0.041, r25 0.035 → 0.037 and qInner 0.035 → 0.039, and `spiral` r90 0.048 → 0.055, each a 1.5 × p95 of a spread that grew when the stand-ins for v21 replaced a second engine draw. These are known gaps of the structure gate at the zoom camera, not hidden ones; the `lines` captures and the breaks (ADR 0020) are what see the line-work. A full pass of the controls would settle the small cells; it is the first thing to run when CI time or a quiet machine allows.
- Engines for new families need replays of their own discrete choices before their goldens mean anything, as here for the line-work.
